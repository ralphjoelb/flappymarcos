PRIVACY POLICY

What we collect

  • Leaderboard entry (only if you choose to save a score): the display name you type (max 18 characters), your score, and a timestamp. Names are shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Rate limiting: your IP address is used to count submissions per minute. The counter is deleted after about 60 seconds.
  • Analytics: the site loads Vercel's Web Analytics script, which collects page-view/visit information. See Vercel's privacy documentation for what Vercel processes.
  • Local storage only: your best score, longest flight, offline leaderboard, and settings are stored in your browser and never sent to us.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no emails, no advertising identifiers, no precise location, no tracking cookies.

Retention

All-time leaderboard: top 100 entries kept. Daily leaderboard: expires after 48 hours. Rate-limit counters: about 60 seconds.

Third-party processors

Vercel — hosting, serverless functions, Web Analytics.
Upstash — leaderboard storage (Redis).
