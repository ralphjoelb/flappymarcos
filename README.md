# FLAPPY MARCOS

> *Avoid accountability. Stay airborne.*

A Flappy Bird-style political satire game of contemporary Philippine governance.
Everything is falling apart — dodge flood-control projects, fuel price hikes,
electricity rates, bureaucracy and paperwork. Keep flying. Avoid accountability.

## Play

Serve the folder with any static server and open it in a browser:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

## Controls

- **Tap / Click / Space** — flap
- **Esc / P** — pause
- **Space** at a press conference — deflect, obviously

## The satire

- Flood-control walls with "PROJECT COMPLETED ✓" signs on visibly broken concrete
- A peso coin that sinks lower the longer it's on screen, with a bearish ₱/$ chart
- GASOLINE at ₱80/L and electricity at ₱14/kWh — prices ticking up live while you fly
- Blackouts that cut the music to an electrical buzz (only Marcos's silhouette remains, then *click*, power returns)
- Rising floodwater, a looming ACCOUNTABILITY slab, and press conferences where
  deflecting is always the correct answer
- Briefcases full of cash (+5). Definitely above board.

All headlines, statements, prices and projects are fictional. A work of political
satire — no real quotations are attributed to any real person.
